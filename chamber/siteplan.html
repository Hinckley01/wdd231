Rookies – Gaming & Esports Community

W05 Project: Site Plan

1. Site Name

Site Name: Rookies

The name Rookies was chosen to represent new and growing gamers who want to learn, improve, and compete in a welcoming esports community.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Rookies website is to provide information about a gaming and esports community. The site will introduce the community, share upcoming events and tournaments, and encourage users to join by providing membership and contact information.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • How can a new gamer find beginner-friendly tournaments and events to join?
  • How can an interested visitor learn about the benefits of becoming a Rookies community member?

4. Color Schema

  • Dark Blue (#0f172a): Used for headers, footer, and primary text.
  • Green (#22c55e): Used for section headings, highlights, and accents.
  • Light Blue (#38bdf8): Used for links and interactive elements.

5. Typography

Primary Font: Segoe UI / Arial (sans-serif)

This font will be used throughout the website for readability and a modern gaming-friendly appearance. Headings are bolded and larger, while body text remains clean and simple.
